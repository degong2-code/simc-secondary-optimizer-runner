## Findings
1. **Tier tokens** are grouped by four armor types, but **the resulting actual tier item IDs and names differ by class**. For example, in our already pinned original SimC fixtures the plate head of Warrior is `271456` (`tempered_horns_of_the_jade_warlord`), whereas the plate head of Paladin is `271465` (`warhelm_of_the_consecrated_flame`). Manage 13 class-tier sets × five potential effective slots (head/shoulders/chest/hands/legs) as class data; the active spec supplies the matching SimC-owned 2pc/4pc effects, not a separately authored copy of each item per spec.
2. **Catalyst as of patch 12.1**: An eligible converted piece keeps the source item's secondary/tertiary stats and **all of the original item's special effects (user-confirmed product rule), including its cantrips**, while it becomes a tier-counting piece at one of the five eligible functional slots. For every **catalyst-eligible** chosen special-effect item, preserve **all original special effects**, rather than whitelisting or removing only some. This is the user's latest direct design ruling. Eligibility of a source piece for catalyst conversion remains a separate inventory/input constraint; SimC effect/DPS semantics remain SimC-owned, with native execution errors reported without a new proc-certification gate. Public documentation directly confirms retention of all cantrips and other stated inherited fields; claiming independent, item-by-item Native verification of every conceivable effect would go beyond those sources. Blizzard official notes: https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24293281 ; Wowhead: https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/matrix-catalyst-crafting-tier-set ; Method: https://www.method.gg/guides/catalyst-stat-changes-in-wow-midnight-season-2
3. **Native SimC serialization:** SimC addon emits `redirected_base_stats=<source_item_id>` on converted items. This is an item modifier (reported as modifier 64); a tier ID without correct source redirected stats can wrongly use native tier secondaries and lose the converted item's stat attribution. See https://github.com/simulationcraft/simc-addon/blob/master/core.lua ; **not** sufficient to invent effect attributes or override rating twice. Existing source fixtures in our repo already contain `redirected_base_stats` on class-tier item lines. Preserve source and exact converted-tier identity/options in the eventual one-slot schema.
4. **Crafted armor** is not eligible for catalyst conversion under the current season's rules; only source-eligible seasonal armor can become a tier-counting slot. A converted special item **occupies one physical slot**, but may simultaneously have fixed special L, retained original special effect(s) and count as ONE tier piece. Never count two pieces or double-add its static L. A user-supplied crafted embellishment occupying a functional tier slot does not count as tier.

## Slot arithmetic (no gear recommendation or automated combo search)
`TIER_SLOTS={head,shoulders,chest,hands,legs}` and `required_effective_tier_count=4`. A slot contributes if it holds a native class-tier item **or a correctly modeled eligible catalyzed item of that class**; occupied crafted slots never contribute. At most **one** of the five functional tier slots can be taken by a non-tier crafted piece if a 4-set is required. Two crafted items inside those five leave only three functioning slots and should return a clear infeasible reason. Normally place embellishment-bearing crafted items in nonfunctional armor slots or weapons as selected, subject to exact physical slot and user-special-fixed choices. Do not automatically reselect different user-special effects/gear to repair an infeasible choice.

Example allowed: converted special head (count 1 and preserve all original special effects + fixed L), native tier shoulders/chest/hands (count 3), regular non-tier legs. Example blocked: crafted embellished head and chest, leaving shoulders/hands/legs only 3 possible tier contributions.
