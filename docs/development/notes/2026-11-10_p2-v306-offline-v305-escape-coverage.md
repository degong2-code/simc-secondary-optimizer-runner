3. **Geometric exclusion in v305:** v305 pairwise bounds the L1/2 simplex distance to its *current region center* by `round(B * radius_fraction * 1.5)`, initially **303**. A direct 373-rating 12%B pairwise step (or **746** for 24%B) from that center fails the initial eligibility check. Its 6-grid proposals can nevertheless have **404-rating L1/2** moves because they change multiple coordinates; therefore it would be wrong to say *all* candidate movement is capped at 303.
